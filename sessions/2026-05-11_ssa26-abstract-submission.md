## Final submitted title

## Final abstract notes
- Reframed the work away from a policy-facing "drought alert" emphasis and toward a soil-science contribution.
- Kept the core methodological contribution centred on AWRA-L v7 root-zone soil moisture percentile rank (`sm_pct`) for spatially and seasonally comparable monitoring.
- Retained the integrated stress formulation using soil moisture, SILO maximum temperature, and SILO vapour pressure deficit.
- Used the South West Agricultural Zone March 2026 run as the representative result window.
- Verified the reported March 2026 result against run outputs in `outputs/risk_2026_mar_SWAZ_boundary_2026-03-26/`:
  - Watch: 45.6%
  - Alert: 15.4%
  - Watch + Alert: 61.0%
  - Time window: 2026-03-01 to 2026-03-24
- Confirmed late-March rainfall relief associated with TC Narelle over SWAZ using SILO daily rainfall; main rainfall pulse occurred 27-30 March 2026, peaking on 28 March. This supported keeping the March window as a pre-relief stress snapshot rather than broadening to a longer Dec-Mar aggregate.
